WHAT IS TALMEEDO?

Talmeedo or Talmido in Syriac (ܬܰܠܡܝܼܕܳܐ) means disciple. We exist to unite men across our Orthodox communities through athletics, brotherhood, and spiritual growth.

TALMEEDO MEN'S MINISTRY

"Come, ye sinners, and implore, seek forgiveness here. To one knocking at the door openeth the Lord. He that asketh doth receive, he that seeketh, he shall find."

The Movement

The Northeast American Diocese has started a movement—calling men from all ages, all walks of life, and all vocations—to join a movement for our sons and daughters, for our girlfriends, for our wives—for ourselves—to transform ourselves to be who we are called to be—HOLY—to be Men for Christ.

Talmeedo—a Syriac word (ܬܰܠܡܝܼܕܳܐ) meaning disciple—is a ministry of our Diocese designed to dedicate/consecrate men to Christ—so we can ensure the world does not define who we are called to be, but to align ourselves to what Christ has called us to be. Talmeedo ministry looks to the examples of the Holy Apostles, men with different characteristics, from different walks of life, with different personalities, as they permitted Christ to transform them into extraordinary men.

Being an Orthodox Christian man in today's world is tough. There are struggles, addictions, challenges, and temptations, which can make us stray from Christ, ruin relationships, or even worse—destroy us and those around us. This ministry will help strengthen our men and edify them as a teenager, college student, a single guy, a single parent, a professional, a young husband, a new father, or a struggling parent.

About Us

  • Talmeedo Men's Ministry strives to transform men ages 17-55 of the Northeast American Diocese into disciples of Christ by strengthening them through brotherhood and engaging in the Scriptural, Sacramental, and Ascetic Life of the Church.
  • Talmeedo aims to draw from the sacred teachings of the Orthodox Christian faith which teaches us that God calls men to seek first the Kingdom of Heaven, obtain virtues, and purity of heart.

Our Goals

  • Regular gatherings of men to foster relationships and vibrant lives that lead them toward the Truth of the Church through Prayer, Holy Scriptures, and the Holy Sacraments.
  • To mentor the next generation of men to understand the role of a brother, father, and son in the Orthodox Church.
  • To serve the community and help bear the crosses of those who are struggling in their daily lives.

Fellowships & Retreats

Talmeedo has monthly men’s fellowships the first Friday of every month in Philadelphia, and the Brooklyn/Queens/Long Island area, where fruitful discussion and comradery is strengthened. In addition to the monthly fellowships, there are seminars, and conferences that take place which address the struggles and concerns of men. A popular ministry of Talmeedo has also been our annual summer retreat at our Holy Transfiguration Retreat Center.

Join the movement across the Diocese. As men from all ages, and backgrounds have decided to take a stand and to answer the call on who we are truly called to be as—sons, husbands, fathers, brothers—Men for Christ.
